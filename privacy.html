Wholefit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Wholefit is a fitness and nutrition app. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it is kept, and how you can delete it. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who we are

Wholefit is run by an independent developer in Pakistan. Contact: fitdessi.app@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

DataWhy
Email addressTo sign you in (we email a one-time code) and back up your data.
Profile: date of birth, sex, height, weight, goal, pace, activity level, training preferences, health notes you choose to addTo work out your calorie, protein, water and step targets safely.
Food, water, weight, waist, sleep and workouts you logTo show your progress and adjust your plan.
Steps from your phone's step counterTo count your daily activity, including when the app is closed.
Health Connect data you allow: steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, weight, exerciseTo use readings from your watch or ring and to save your workouts back to Health Connect.
Location during a run, walk or ride you startTo measure distance and draw your route. Only while that activity is running.
Messages to the AI coach, meal descriptions and meal photosTo answer you. Sent to our server and to Google's Gemini AI to produce the answer.
Waitlist email (website)To email you once when the app launches. You can ask us to remove it at any time.
Feedback you send and short crash notes (error, app version, phone type)To fix problems. Crash notes do not include your food, weight or health readings.

Progress photos stay on your phone and are not uploaded. Voice typing uses your phone's own speech service (Google or Apple); Wholefit receives only the text.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Supabase (database and sign-in), servers in Singapore. Your rows can only be read by your own account.
  • Google Gemini API (AI answers). We use the paid service, under which Google does not use the content to train its models.
  • Google Play distributes the app.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure storage.

Health Connect

Wholefit's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use it only to provide the fitness features you see in the app; we never use it for advertising, never sell it, and never share it except as needed to run the app (the storage provider above).

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is deleted from our database straight away and from backups within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Use the app without signing in: data stays on your phone only.
  • Turn off any permission (steps, location, Health Connect, microphone, camera) in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Delete your account and data: in the app, Settings → Delete account, or see how to delete your data.
  • Ask for a copy of your data: email us.

Age

Wholefit is for people aged 16 and over. Weight-loss plans for people under 18 use a gentler pace.

Not medical advice

Wholefit gives general fitness and nutrition guidance, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, or have a history of eating disorders.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and tell you in the app for important changes.
