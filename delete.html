Delete your Thrive account and data

Thrive · fitness and nutrition app

In the app (fastest): open Thrive → Settings → Delete account → confirm. Everything is deleted straight away.
Without the app: email fitdessi.app@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Thrive account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your account, email address, profile, all logs (food, water, weight, sleep, steps, workouts, routes), AI coach messages, feedback and crash notes. Copies in backups are removed within 30 days.

What is kept

Nothing. Your daily AI-use counts are deleted with your account.
